- **Visual Layout:** Dynamic 3-stage loop: (1) Ingest Overnight HRV/Sleep $\rightarrow$ (2) Auto-Modulate Workout Load $\rightarrow$ (3) Edge Audio Coaching during movement execution.
- **Talking Points:** *"FitBuddy is not a static PDF or chatbot. It is a closed-loop coaching engine. If your HRV indicates central nervous system fatigue, FitBuddy scales back volume by 30%. When you perform squats, our on-device vision counts reps and corrects depth in real time."*
